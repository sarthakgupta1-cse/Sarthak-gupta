About us

A small press with a wide shelf.

Oakridge Media is an independent publishing company in the United States. We make books for readers of every age and publish them worldwide through Amazon.

Oakridge Media was founded in 2026 with a simple idea: a good book should be easy to find, pleasant to hold and worth the money. We publish across categories because a household's reading does too. A parent might buy a bedtime picture book, a novel for themselves and a planner for the year ahead, and each of those deserves the same attention.

We work with writers, illustrators and designers to plan each title around its reader. Children's books are tested for read-aloud rhythm and age-appropriate language. Fiction is edited for pace and clarity. Non-fiction is organized so readers can find what they need. Journals and puzzle books are checked page by page before they go to print.

Our books are printed on demand and distributed by Amazon through Kindle Direct Publishing. That means they don't sit in warehouses, they are rarely out of stock, and readers in the US and many other countries can order them easily.
