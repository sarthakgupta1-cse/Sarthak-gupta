Privacy Policy

How Oakridge Media collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website or contact us.

Effective November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Oakridge Media LLC ("Oakridge Media", "we", "us") handles personal information collected through this website. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. If you email us or use our contact form, we receive your name, email address and the contents of your message.
  • Technical information. Our website host may automatically log basic data such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited, to keep the site secure and working.

Purchases

We do not sell books directly on this website. When you buy one of our books, you buy it from Amazon, and Amazon's own privacy notice governs the information you give them. We do not receive your payment details.

How we use information

  • To answer your questions and requests.
  • To run, secure and improve this website.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the website or handle email (for example a hosting or form provider), when the law requires it, or to protect our rights.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising cookies. Our host or font provider may use essential technical cookies or logs. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy.

Children's privacy

We publish books for children, but this website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, including California, you may have additional rights under state law. Email us to make a request.

Data retention and security

We keep messages only as long as needed to respond and for our records, and we take reasonable steps to protect them. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
