How a book gets made

From first idea to your doorstep.

  1. Find the reader

    We start with who the book is for and what they need from it, then shape the idea around them.

  2. Write and design

    Manuscripts, illustrations, interiors and covers are made to fit the age group and the format.

  3. Edit and proof

    Every title is edited, and we order printed proofs to check paper, color and binding by hand.

  4. Publish on Amazon

    Books are printed on demand through Amazon KDP and ship to readers across the US and worldwide.
